MISSION BRIEFING

Outpost EAGLE-7 is under sustained assault. Hold the compound, eliminate all hostile waves, and stay alive as long as you can. Reinforcements drop every 15 seconds between waves — resupply during the lull.

FIELD NOTES

  • Headshots deal double damage. Aim high.
  • Crouching tightens your spread and steadies recoil.
  • Enemies flank. Watch the sector radar in the top-right.
  • Health regenerates after a few seconds out of contact — armour does not.
  • Enemy muzzle flashes give away their position. Fire in short bursts.
